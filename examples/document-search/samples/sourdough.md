# Baking Sourdough Bread

Sourdough bread is made through the slow fermentation of dough using wild lactobacilli and yeast.

## Ingredients
- 500g strong bread flour
- 350g lukewarm water (70% hydration)
- 100g active sourdough starter (at peak rise)
- 10g fine sea salt

## The Process
1. **Autolyse**: Mix flour and water together and let rest for 30–60 minutes to develop gluten and improve extensibility.
2. **Incorporate Starter & Salt**: Add the active sourdough starter and salt, folding until thoroughly combined.
3. **Bulk Fermentation**: Ferment at room temperature (75°F / 24°C) for 4 to 5 hours, performing stretch-and-fold sets every 30 minutes during the first 2 hours.
4. **Shaping**: Preshape into a round boule, bench rest for 20 minutes, then final shape and place in a dusted banneton.
5. **Cold Retard**: Refrigerate overnight at 38°F (3°C) for 12–16 hours to develop complex lactic and acetic acid flavors.
6. **Baking**: Preheat a Dutch oven to 450°F (230°C). Score the dough with a razor blade (lame) and bake covered with steam for 20 minutes, then uncovered for 20–25 minutes until the crust is deep golden brown and crackling.
