Changing a Flat Tire Roadside Safety Guide

1. Safe Location: Pull over to a level, stable surface well away from moving traffic. Turn on hazard emergency flashers and firmly engage the parking brake.
2. Wheel Chocks: Place wheel chocks or heavy rocks behind the opposite tires to prevent rolling.
3. Loosen Lug Nuts: While the vehicle is still firmly on the ground, use the lug wrench to break the initial torque on the lug nuts by turning counterclockwise half a turn. Do not remove them yet.
4. Position the Jack: Place the hydraulic or scissor jack under the designated metal jacking point on the vehicle's frame (consult your owner manual).
5. Raise Vehicle: Carefully pump or crank the jack until the flat tire clears the pavement by approximately six inches.
6. Swap Wheel: Fully remove the loosened lug nuts. Pull the flat wheel straight off the hub. Mount the spare tire onto the wheel studs and screw the lug nuts on finger-tight.
7. Lower and Torque: Lower the vehicle until the tire touches the ground without bearing full vehicle weight. Use the lug wrench to tighten the nuts in a crisscross or star pattern. Lower fully to the ground and finish torquing to manufacturer specification.
